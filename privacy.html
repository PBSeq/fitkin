fitkin privacy policy

Effective: August 2026. fitkin is operated by PathoBrainSeq (contact: pathobrainseq@gmail.com).

what we collect

  • Your first name, the sports you pick, your training preferences, the days you're available, and the ZIP code you type.
  • If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from that provider, used only to keep your account signed in across devices. Guest mode collects neither.
  • If you choose to add a profile photo or share a workout moment, we store that photo and show it to other fitkin users — your profile photo appears wherever your card appears, and workout moments are visible to users near your ZIP area. Photos are screened automatically on your device before upload whenever the screening model is available, every photo can be reported in one tap, and every report is reviewed by a human within 24 hours, and you can delete your photos (and your whole account) in the app at any time.
  • That's it. We do not collect your phone number, your contacts, or your precise location. We never access GPS.

what we do with it

  • Your kin card (name, sports, preferences, ZIP code) is visible to other fitkin users near your ZIP — that visibility is the product: it's how kin find each other.
  • Kin connections happen when you deliberately share your kin code (QR), and messages are private between the two of you.
  • The QR scanner uses the camera only to read kin codes, entirely on your device — scanner frames are never uploaded or saved. Photos enter fitkin only when you deliberately choose one for your profile or a workout moment.
  • You can block any kin (they can no longer message you) and report anyone from inside a chat.

what we never do

  • No selling data. No ads. No trackers or analytics SDKs.
  • No precise location: we store only the 5-digit ZIP code you choose to type, and other users see just its first 3 digits.

storage & deletion

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, US). To delete your account, tap "delete my profile" inside the app — it removes your card and your sign-in from our servers immediately. You can also email pathobrainseq@gmail.com from any address with your first name and ZIP; we delete within 7 days.

community rules

  • fitkin is for finding workout partners. harassment, hate, sexual content, spam, or using fitkin for anything other than meeting people to train with gets your profile removed.
  • every report is reviewed by a human. blocked users can never message you again — enforced on our servers, not just your phone.
  • meet smart: first workouts in public places, tell a friend where you're going.

waitlist

The waitlist on our website is handled by Gumroad and collects your email under Gumroad's privacy policy. We use it only to invite you when fitkin opens in your area.
