fitkin privacy policy

Effective: August 2026. fitkin is operated by PathoBrainSeq (contact: pathobrainseq@gmail.com).

what we collect

  • Your first name, the sports you pick, your training preferences, the days you're available, and the neighborhood name you type.
  • That's it. We do not collect your email in the app, your phone number, your contacts, or your precise location. We never access GPS.

what we do with it

  • Your kin card (name, sports, preferences, ZIP code) is visible to other fitkin users near your ZIP — that visibility is the product: it's how kin find each other.
  • Kin connections happen when you deliberately share your kin code (QR), and messages are private between the two of you.
  • The camera is used only while you scan a kin code. No photos are taken or stored, and nothing leaves your device.
  • You can block any kin (they can no longer message you) and report anyone from inside a chat.

what we never do

  • No selling data. No ads. No trackers or analytics SDKs.
  • No precise location: we store only the neighborhood text you choose to type.

storage & deletion

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, US). To delete your profile, email pathobrainseq@gmail.com from any address with your first name and neighborhood, or use in-app deletion when it ships in the next version. We delete within 7 days.

community rules

  • fitkin is for finding workout partners. harassment, hate, sexual content, spam, or using fitkin for anything other than meeting people to train with gets your profile removed.
  • every report is reviewed by a human. blocked users can never message you again — enforced on our servers, not just your phone.
  • meet smart: first workouts in public places, tell a friend where you're going.

waitlist

The waitlist on our website is handled by Gumroad and collects your email under Gumroad's privacy policy. We use it only to invite you when fitkin opens in your area.
