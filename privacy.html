fitkin privacy policy

Effective: August 2026. fitkin is operated by PathoBrainSeq (contact: pathobrainseq@gmail.com).

what we collect

  • Your first name, the sports you pick, your training preferences, the days you're available, and the neighborhood name you type.
  • That's it. We do not collect your email in the app, your phone number, your contacts, or your precise location. We never access GPS.

what we do with it

  • Your profile (name, sports, preferences, neighborhood) is visible to other fitkin users — that visibility is the product: it's how kin find each other.
  • Kin connections happen only when you deliberately share your kin code (QR). We never connect people automatically.

what we never do

  • No selling data. No ads. No trackers or analytics SDKs.
  • No precise location: we store only the neighborhood text you choose to type.

storage & deletion

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, US). To delete your profile, email pathobrainseq@gmail.com from any address with your first name and neighborhood, or use in-app deletion when it ships in the next version. We delete within 7 days.

waitlist

The waitlist on our website is handled by Gumroad and collects your email under Gumroad's privacy policy. We use it only to invite you when fitkin opens in your area.
